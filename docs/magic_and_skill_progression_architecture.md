Magic & Skill Progression Architecture

Target Audience: Lead Coding Agent / Systems Architect
Context: This document outlines how the Player (and advanced NPCs) progress in power. We explicitly reject abstract "Experience Points" and pre-defined "Skill Trees." Progression in "The Living Delve" is diegetic: it is gained through physical repetition, biological mutation, material wealth, and the excavation of ancient knowledge (driven by the DAG).

1. Core Philosophy: Excavating Power

You do not "level up" and suddenly know how to cast a fireball.

Knowledge is Physical/Historical: Spells and martial techniques are ancient knowledge. To learn a Dwarven Magma-Shaping spell, you must find a ruined Dwarven library (placed by the DAG history generator), translate it, or convince an LLM-driven Tier 3 Faction Leader to teach it to you.

Modular Magic (The Syntax of Reality): Magic is not a list of hardcoded spells. It is a node-based programming language that manipulates the ECS Chemistry Engine.

Muscle Memory: Physical skills improve through the ECS registering repeated successful actions ([Action: Swing Sword] -> increases Blade_Familiarity float).

2. The Modular Spellcrafting System

Magic involves constructing an EphemeralEntity (a temporary ECS entity) that manipulates the world's state. Spells are composed of "Runes" (data components) that the player arranges in their GrimoireUI.

Spell Components (The Runes)

A spell requires at least three components to function:

Trigger (When does it happen?):

Examples: On_Cast, On_Impact, On_Timer(3s), On_Proximity.

Shape/Delivery (How does it move?):

Examples: Projectile(Speed, Mass), Cone(Angle, Range), Aura(Radius), Self.

Catalyst (What ECS tags does it alter?):

Examples: Add_Temperature(500), Apply_Tag[Kinetic_Force], Remove_Tag[Wet], Apply_Tag[Fungal_Spores].

Emergent Examples

Standard Fireball: On_Cast + Projectile(High) + On_Impact + Add_Temperature(1000).

The "Trap": On_Proximity + Aura(Small) + Apply_Tag[Kinetic_Force(Upwards)]. (Creates a physical landmine that launches whoever steps on it).

The "Ecosystem Nuke": On_Cast + Projectile + On_Impact + Apply_Tag[Filth(Massive)]. (Does no direct damage, but instantly corrupts a room, causing the EcologySystem to spawn a swarm of scavengers over the next few simulation ticks).

The Cost of Magic

There is no standard regenerating "Mana" bar. Magic requires fuel.

Material Cost: Some spells require consuming physical items from the InventoryComponent (e.g., bone dust, sulfur).

Biological Strain: Channeling raw ECS state-changes causes "Strain," represented as temporary damage to the caster's Max_Stamina or Max_Health components until they rest in a safe zone.

3. Diegetic Skill Progression (Physical & Mental)

Skills are data floats attached to the Player's MindComponent and BodyComponent.

Muscle Memory (Action-Driven Growth)

When the ActionResolutionSystem processes a physical act, it slightly increments a hidden float.

Example: Successfully blocking a heavy attack increases the Block_Efficiency multiplier. Over time, the player uses less stamina to block the same amount of kinetic force.

Decay: To prevent grinding against a wall, skills have a soft cap based on the player's current physical state, requiring greater challenges to push the number higher.

Insight & Lore (Knowledge-Driven Growth)

The world is filled with unknown entities. The player has a Bestiary/InsightComponent.

Anatomical Study: Killing and butchering a Tier 2 Spider entity yields physical parts, but also increases the player's Insight[Arachnid]. High insight reveals weak points (increasing critical hit multipliers against that specific tag) and reveals their ECS data in the Tactical Lens UI.

Language & Culture: Finding historical tablets (generated by the DAG) increases fluency in dead or active languages. This is required to read spell runes or converse (via LLM) with deep-dungeon factions without angering them due to mistranslation.

4. Biological Mutation (The Dark Path)

Because the world simulates ecology (Filth, Spores, Radiation/Magic Fallout), the player's BodyComponent is malleable.

Exposure: Spending too much time in a [High_Filth] zone without proper filter equipment (mask) forces a roll against the EcologySystem.

The Result: The player might contract a disease (a component that drains stamina), OR they might mutate.

Mutations as Progression: A player might gain Tag[Night_Vision] but permanently lose Charisma (making trade with the Surface Village more expensive). This turns the environment itself into a dangerous, unpredictable skill tree.

5. System Architecture (Godot & ECS Handshake)

Godot UI: The GrimoireUI allows drag-and-drop connecting of Rune Items. When the player clicks "Compile Spell," Godot validates the graph and generates a JSON payload.

ECS Interpreter: The ECS receives the payload, verifies the player has the required Runes/Knowledge in their MindComponent, and registers a new Action_ID.

When the player presses 'Cast', Godot sends the Action_ID to the ECS JobQueue, and the ECS handles the physics, spawning the EphemeralEntity and resolving the chemistry tags.
